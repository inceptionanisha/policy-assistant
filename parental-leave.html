Parental Leave

Paid parental leave after the birth or adoption of a child. Amount depends on full-time or part-time status.

Full-time employees

16 weeks paid. Must be taken within 12 months of the child's arrival. Submit the request in the HR portal 30 days before the start date, or as soon as possible if unplanned.

Part-time employees

8 weeks paid, prorated to scheduled hours. Must be taken within 12 months of the child's arrival.
