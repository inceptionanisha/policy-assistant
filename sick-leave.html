Sick Leave

Paid sick days per calendar year. Notify your manager before your shift starts.

Full-time employees

10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for absences over 3 consecutive days. Notify your manager before your shift starts.

Part-time employees

Prorated to contracted hours. A 0.5 FTE employee receives 5 paid sick days per year. Notify your manager before your shift starts.
