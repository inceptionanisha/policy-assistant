Annual Leave (PTO)

Paid annual leave accrues each year based on contracted hours. Up to 5 unused days carry over.

Full-time employees

20 days per year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 days carry over to the next year and must be used by March 31.

Part-time employees

Accrues in proportion to contracted hours. A 0.5 FTE employee accrues 10 days per year. The same carry-over rule applies.
